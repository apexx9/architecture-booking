# Microinteractions

Use this skill for small interactions that communicate
feedback, state and responsiveness.

## Components

- Buttons.
- Navigation links.
- Tabs.
- Dropdowns.
- Tooltips.
- Form controls.
- Checkboxes and switches.
- Dialogs.
- Drawers.
- Toast notifications.
- Loading indicators.
- Expandable content.

## Interaction States

Consider:
- Default.
- Hover.
- Focus-visible.
- Active or pressed.
- Disabled.
- Loading.
- Success.
- Error.

Only implement states that are meaningful for the component.

## Principles

- Feedback should be immediate.
- Motion should not delay user actions.
- Use subtle changes in opacity, position or colour.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus.
- Do not rely on hover alone.
- Avoid exaggerated scaling and bouncing.
- Avoid animating large surfaces unnecessarily.

## Business Application UX

Renove's workspace should prioritise clarity and speed.

Keep table, form and navigation interactions quick.
Avoid decorative motion in repetitive workflows.

Never invent business states or interactions.

Follow MOTION.md.
